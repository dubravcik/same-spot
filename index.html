Same Spot

Retake an old photo from the same spot

Load the old picture, hold it over your camera and shoot when the outlines meet. The new photo is then fitted onto the old one, so you can slide between the two.

Finding the spotPick a near edge and a far edge that touch in the old photo, a lamp post against a tower for example. Step sideways until they touch the same way. Aim and zoom can be fixed afterwards, where you stand cannot.

Live camera is not available in this view. Take the shot with your phone camera, matching this framing, then line the two up in the next step.

The numbered rings sit on distinctive details of the old photo. Drag one to pick a better detail. A dashed ring shows where that detail is now. Move until it sits in the solid ring.

Zoom, automatic 1.0×

Drag each numbered mark onto the same detail in both photos, such as a window corner or a roof tip. Keep the four marks far apart.
